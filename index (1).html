Annual change toward E or W is the way the variation moves each year. A chart bracket such as (8′E) is toward E. "Decreasing" on a West variation is also toward E. ECDIS shows West as negative, so a variation of −4° with a change of −7′ is 4°W moving 7′ a year toward W.
